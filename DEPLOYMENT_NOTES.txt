SNIPIVO 1.1 - TIKTOK + INSTAGRAM
Date: 2026-09-18

BASE RECOVERY
- The uploaded ZIP was confirmed to be the "Add IndexNow integration" commit.
- That commit had accidentally deleted app/static/styles.css and app/static/app.js.
- This package was rebuilt from the previous stable commit:
  "Improve download loading and prevent repeated clicks".
- IndexNow was then re-added safely WITHOUT deleting the CSS or JavaScript assets.

NEW INSTAGRAM SUPPORT
- Auto-detect TikTok vs Instagram URLs.
- Public Instagram Reels supported when yt-dlp can resolve them without login.
- Public single Instagram video posts supported when yt-dlp can resolve them without login.
- Instagram MP4 download.
- Instagram MP3 extraction.
- TikTok Clean / MP4 / MP3 flow preserved.
- "Without watermark" is TikTok-only and hidden for Instagram.
- Private/login-only Instagram content returns a user-friendly error.
- Instagram image-only posts, profiles, stories and carousels are intentionally unsupported in this release.
- No Instagram password/cookies are requested or stored.

AFTER DEPLOYMENT - TEST THESE FIRST
1. Open https://snipivo.online and hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R).
2. Confirm the premium dark UI is intact.
3. Test one known public TikTok link: resolve, Clean (if available), MP4, MP3.
4. Test one public Instagram Reel you own or are allowed to save: resolve, MP4, MP3.
5. Test one public Instagram single video post.
6. Test a private/login-only Instagram link and confirm a clean error appears.
7. Confirm /api/health returns ok=true and lists tiktok + instagram.
8. Confirm /instagram-video-downloader/ and /instagram-reels-downloader/ load with styling.
9. After the site is confirmed healthy, submit ONLY the two new Instagram URLs through IndexNow if desired.

IMPORTANT
Instagram can change its public delivery/login requirements. A public post can sometimes still be rejected by Instagram/yt-dlp. The app fails gracefully rather than using personal account credentials to bypass those restrictions.
