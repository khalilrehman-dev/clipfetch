How the Instagram video downloader works

Snipivo accepts a single public Instagram video-post link and checks the media formats exposed for that post. When Instagram makes the video publicly resolvable, Snipivo can prepare a best-available MP4 or extract MP3 audio.

1Copy a public post link

Open the Instagram video post and use Copy link.

2Paste it into Snipivo

Snipivo detects Instagram automatically and does not ask for your password.

3Choose your file

Select MP4 for video or MP3 when you are allowed to save the audio.

What is supported?

Public Reels and public video posts remain supported. Snipivo now also has best-effort support for public single-image posts and image carousels. Private posts, login-only content, stories, and profiles are not supported.

Why can a public Instagram link fail?

Instagram can require login for some public pages, change its delivery system, remove a post, apply regional restrictions, or rate-limit automated requests. Snipivo does not ask for your Instagram credentials to bypass those restrictions.

Does Snipivo store Instagram videos?

Download artifacts are prepared in temporary server storage and scheduled for deletion after the response is sent. Snipivo is not intended to be a permanent media-hosting service.

Use responsibly. Download only videos you own, created yourself, have permission to save, or are otherwise legally entitled to download.
