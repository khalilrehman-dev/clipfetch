Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about how Snipivo works and the limits users should know before downloading public media.

Is Snipivo free to use?

Snipivo currently provides the public TikTok download utility without requiring an account.

Can Snipivo download private TikTok videos?

No. Snipivo is designed for public links and does not use private account sessions or login bypasses.

Does Snipivo remove a watermark from video pixels?

No. It looks for a clean public media stream when the source platform exposes one. It does not edit frames to erase a watermark.

Why is the clean download option sometimes unavailable?

Not every public post exposes the same media formats. When a clean stream cannot be identified, Snipivo disables that option rather than pretending a clean version exists.

What is Best available MP4?

It is a fallback video option that asks the backend to retrieve the best compatible MP4-style output it can prepare from the available public formats.

What does Download MP3 do?

It extracts the audio from the permitted public media and prepares an MP3 file. Only use it when you have the right to save or reuse the underlying audio.

Does Snipivo permanently store my downloads?

No. Download artifacts are processed in temporary server storage and are scheduled for deletion after delivery.

Why can a link stop working?

Source platforms frequently change media delivery, anti-bot systems, rate limits, and formats. A link may also be removed, made private, region-limited, or otherwise unavailable.

Is Snipivo affiliated with TikTok?

No. Snipivo is an independent utility and is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok or ByteDance.

What content may I download?

Only content you own, created yourself, have permission to save, or are otherwise legally entitled to download. You are responsible for your use of downloaded material.
