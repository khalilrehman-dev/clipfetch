About Snipivo

A simple utility for permitted public media.

Snipivo is an independent web utility designed to help people save public TikTok media they own or have permission to download.

What Snipivo does

Paste a public TikTok link and Snipivo checks the media formats that are publicly available for that post. Depending on what TikTok exposes, Snipivo can offer a clean video stream, a best-available MP4 option, or MP3 audio.

What Snipivo does not do

Snipivo does not log in to private accounts, bypass private or login-only posts, or erase a watermark from video pixels. If a clean public stream is not available, the service says so.

Temporary processing

Media files created for downloads are processed in temporary server storage and are scheduled for deletion after the response is sent. Snipivo is not designed as a permanent media-hosting service.

Independent service

Snipivo is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by TikTok or ByteDance. TikTok is a trademark of its respective owner.
