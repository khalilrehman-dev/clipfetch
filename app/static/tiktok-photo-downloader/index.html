How it works

1Copy the photo-post link

Open the public TikTok photo post and copy its share URL.

2Paste into Snipivo

Snipivo detects photo media when the public post exposes it.

3Download

Save the available image or download supported multi-image posts together.

Availability

TikTok can change public delivery endpoints, require app/login access for some posts, or rate-limit servers. Photo support is therefore best-effort and works only when the public post exposes image files.

Use responsibly. Save only media you own, have permission to download, or are otherwise legally entitled to keep.
