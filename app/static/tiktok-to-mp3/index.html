What TikTok to MP3 does

The MP3 option extracts the audio track from the public media Snipivo can resolve and prepares an audio-only file. It is useful when you need the audio from your own clip, a recording you have permission to use, or another legally permitted source.

How to create an MP3

1Copy the public TikTok URL

Use the Share menu on the post.

2Paste and resolve

Snipivo checks the available media information.

3Select Download MP3

The backend prepares an audio-only file for download.

MP3 quality

The current Snipivo interface prepares an MP3 output aimed at broad device compatibility. Converting a source to MP3 does not improve the original audio quality; the result is limited by the source media.

Audio rights matter too

Music, voice recordings, podcasts, and other audio can be protected by copyright even when they appear inside a public video. Only download or reuse audio when you have the necessary rights or permission.

Tip: If you need the complete video rather than only its sound, use the clean video or best available MP4 option instead.
