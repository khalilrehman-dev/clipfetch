How to download TikTok videos on iPhone

Use Safari to paste a public TikTok link into Snipivo, choose a format, and locate the downloaded file on your iPhone.

Download a permitted TikTok video on iPhone

1Copy link

In TikTok, open Share and choose Copy link.

2Open Safari

Visit Snipivo and paste the URL into the downloader.

3Pick a format

Choose clean video, best available MP4, or MP3.

4Open the file

Check Safari Downloads or the Files app.

Where does Safari save the file?

Safari typically places downloaded files in the Downloads location configured for Safari, which is commonly accessible from the Files app. Your exact location can vary depending on iCloud Drive and Safari settings.

How do I move a video into Photos?

If the downloaded video opens from Files, use the iOS share sheet and choose the appropriate save-to-Photos action when available. Some formats may remain in Files depending on the media type and iOS version.

If the download does not start

Check that the TikTok post is public, reload Snipivo, and try the best available MP4 option. Content blockers, temporary network issues, source restrictions, or a source-platform change can also prevent a successful download.

No app install required. Snipivo runs in the browser; you do not need to install a separate iPhone app to use the web downloader.
