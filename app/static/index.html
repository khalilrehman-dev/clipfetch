Good to know

Which Instagram links work?

Public Reels, public video posts, single-image posts, and supported image carousels can work. Private/login-only posts, profiles, and stories are not supported.

Does TikTok “clean” remove a watermark from pixels?

No. Snipivo only offers that option when TikTok itself exposes a suitable public clean stream. It does not edit, blur, crop, or paint over watermarks.

Why can a public link fail sometimes?

TikTok and Instagram can change delivery systems, require login for some posts, or rate-limit servers. Snipivo shows a clear error rather than requesting your social account password.

Are downloads stored permanently?

No. Prepared videos, audio, and image ZIPs are kept only temporarily and expire automatically.
