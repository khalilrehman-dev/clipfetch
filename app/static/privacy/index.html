Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026. This policy explains what information Snipivo processes when you use the website.

1. Information processed to provide the service

When you submit a public TikTok URL, the server processes that URL so it can retrieve publicly available metadata and media formats and prepare the format you request. The URL is used to perform the request; Snipivo does not intentionally include submitted video URLs, creator names, or video titles in its custom Google Analytics events.

2. Temporary media files

Some download formats require temporary server-side files. These files are created for the requested download and are scheduled for deletion after the response is sent. Snipivo is not intended to keep a permanent library of downloaded videos or audio.

3. Technical logs and security

Like most internet services, the hosting infrastructure may process technical information such as IP address, request time, request path, browser or user-agent information, response status, and security or rate-limit data. This information may be used for reliability, abuse prevention, troubleshooting, and security.

4. Google Analytics

Snipivo uses Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate website usage, such as page views, approximate geography, device type, traffic source, and interactions. Snipivo also records aggregate events such as a successful video resolution and which download option was selected. Google may use cookies or similar technologies according to its own terms and privacy practices.

5. Cookies and local technologies

Analytics and future advertising features may use cookies or similar technologies. Where consent is legally required, Snipivo intends to provide the appropriate consent controls. If advertising is added later, this policy will be updated before or when those features are enabled.

6. Sharing and selling

Snipivo does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the website, such as hosting, analytics, DNS, security, and infrastructure providers, subject to their own terms and policies.

7. Data retention

Temporary media files are designed to be short-lived. Hosting and analytics providers may retain technical or analytics records according to their configured retention periods and policies.

8. Children

Snipivo is a general-purpose utility and is not directed specifically to children. Do not submit personal information through the service.

9. Your choices

You can use browser privacy controls to manage cookies and similar technologies. Depending on your location, you may also have legal rights concerning personal data processed about you.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Snipivo changes. The date at the top will be revised when material changes are published.

11. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to support@snipivo.online.
