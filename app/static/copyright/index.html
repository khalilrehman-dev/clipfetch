Copyright and takedown requests

Snipivo is intended for lawful use of public media. Users are required to download only content they own or have permission to save.

Snipivo does not grant content rights

Videos, audio, usernames, trademarks, and other third-party materials remain the property of their respective owners. The availability of a public media stream does not mean the content is free of copyright or other restrictions.

Temporary processing

Snipivo is designed as an on-demand utility rather than a permanent media-hosting library. Download artifacts created by the service are processed temporarily and scheduled for deletion after delivery.

Submitting a notice

If you believe Snipivo is being used in a way that infringes your copyright or other rights, send a notice to support@snipivo.online. Please include:

  • Your full name and a reliable way to contact you.
  • Identification of the copyrighted work or other protected material.
  • The specific public URL involved.
  • A clear explanation of the rights you believe are being infringed.
  • A statement that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the rights holder or authorized to act for the rights holder.

Snipivo may request additional information when reasonably necessary to evaluate a notice and may take appropriate technical or operational action.

Misrepresentations

Please do not submit false, misleading, or abusive notices. If you are unsure whether you own the relevant rights or whether an exception applies, consider obtaining legal advice before sending a notice.
